# L'UGX Brand Context

Brand Category: Nail care and beauty tools

Target Market: United States 

## Product Description

L'UGX specializes in nail and foot care tools, including:

- Electric nail drills
- Nail dust collectors
- UV/LED nail lamps
- Electric foot callus removers
- Other manicure and pedicure tools

Our products are designed to make nail care routines easier, more efficient, and more convenient for both professional nail technicians and DIY beauty enthusiasts.

## Core Audience

- Professional nail technicians
- Nail salon owners
- DIY nail enthusiasts
- Nail art and beauty lovers
- People who enjoy at-home manicures and pedicures

## Influencer Collaboration Strategy

We primarily target creators in the United States.

Our current collaboration strategy focuses on product gifting in exchange for content creation. We do not currently have a regular paid influencer collaboration budget.

We are interested in creators who produce:

- Nail tutorials
- Nail product reviews
- Manicure and pedicure content
- Nail tool demonstrations
- Beauty content relevant to nail care

## Influencer Screening Criteria

When evaluating creators, consider:

1. Content relevance to our products
2. Audience location and demographics
3. Engagement rate and authentic interactions
4. Average views of recent videos
5. Content quality
6. Experience with similar products
7. Product demonstration ability
8. Potential to drive traffic or sales
9. Previous collaborations with competitors
10. Suitability for product gifting collaborations

Do not evaluate creators based on follower count alone.

## Brand Communication Style

Our communication should be:

- Friendly
- Natural
- Professional but not overly formal
- Concise
- Personalized
- Not overly sales-focused

Influencer outreach messages should be short and easy to understand.

## Marketing Goals

- Build brand awareness in the US market
- Find suitable nail and beauty creators
- Generate authentic product demonstrations and reviews
- Drive traffic to our Amazon listings
- Increase product visibility and sales
- Build long-term relationships with creators
